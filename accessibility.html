Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: November 12, 2026

Our commitment

Kestrel Bay Health wants everyone to be able to find care, book visits, and manage their health online, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, voice control, magnification, or other assistive technology.

Conformance target

This website aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

What we have done

  • Built with semantic HTML landmarks (header, navigation, main, footer) and a logical heading structure.
  • Added a "Skip to main content" link as the first item on every page.
  • Made every feature usable with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator on all links, buttons, and form fields.
  • Checked that all text meets a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.
  • Underlined links so they do not rely on color alone.
  • Gave every form field a visible label, and linked error messages to their fields.
  • Made buttons and form fields at least 44 pixels tall.
  • Made pages work at 320 pixels wide and at 200% zoom without horizontal scrolling.
  • Respected the "reduce motion" setting in your operating system.
